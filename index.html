Mochi Journal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: Mochi Journal is a private diary that works entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, share or sell any of your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no internet permission, so it cannot send your journal anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Mochi Journal ("the app") does not collect personal information of any kind, and the developer has no access to your entries, photos or settings.

What stays on your device

  • Your journal entries, moods, tags and settings
  • Photos you attach to entries (copied into the app's private storage)
  • Your app-lock PIN (stored only as a salted hash) and your biometric unlock preference

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or use Delete all data.

Device permissions

  • Notifications – used only to show the daily reminder, and only if you turn it on.
  • Biometrics – used only to unlock the app if you enable fingerprint or face unlock. Android handles this; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Photos – chosen through Android's system photo picker, so the app never gets access to your whole gallery.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking services, and shares no data with any third party.

Backups and exports

  • If Android's cloud backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account according to your device's backup settings. This is controlled by you and Google, not by the app.
  • The backup and export features save a file to a location you choose. Anyone who has that file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Restoring from a backup only reads the file you pick.

Voice typing

If you use the microphone button, the text is produced by your device's own speech-recognition service, which is governed by that service's own privacy policy. Mochi Journal itself never records or stores audio.

Rating and sharing

The "Rate" and "Tell a friend" options hand off to Google Play or to the sharing app you choose. Those apps have their own privacy policies, and nothing from your journal is included.

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries inside the app, use Delete all data in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date.
